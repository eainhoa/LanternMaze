Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lantern Maze. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you may share while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our game.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number directly.

However, the app may use third-party services (such as Google AdMob) that automatically collect certain technical information to provide advertisements and analyze app performance, including:

  • Device type and operating system version.
  • Anonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS).
  • General gameplay interaction data and crash reports.

3. Third-Party Services (AdMob & Analytics)

Lantern Maze integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to our app or other apps on the internet. You can manage your ad tracking preferences directly through your device's privacy settings.

4. Local Data Storage

Game progress, high scores, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device using secure local storage mechanisms. We do not transmit or store your personal game progress data on external remote servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed to be safe for a general audience (Rated 9+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal data has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
