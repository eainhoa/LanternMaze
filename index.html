Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • How do I recover lost coins or purchases? Make sure you are logged into the same App Store account used for the purchase. You can also restart the app.
  • How do I control the flashlight? Use your finger on your screen to navigate the maze and keep your battery charged.
  • Does the game require an internet connection? You can play offline, though an internet connection is required to load optional rewards or updates.
